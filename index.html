04 / Gradient Descent Lab

Watch a red ball move along a function curve. Each step uses the derivative to move x downhill: x = x − learningRate × derivative(x).

How it works: The derivative tells us the local slope. Gradient descent moves x in the opposite direction of that slope. A suitable learning rate helps the ball approach a local minimum; a rate that is too large can make it overshoot or diverge.
